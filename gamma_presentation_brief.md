# OWD Glass-Engine: AI-Powered Glass Quoting System

## SLIDE 1: THE PROBLEM

**The "Free Quote" Crisis in South African Glass Industry**

With petrol exceeding R25/L, a "Free Quote" now costs glass shops approximately R500 in fuel and labor every time a bakkie leaves the shop for a lead that doesn't close.

- Glass shop owners doing quotes at 10 PM, struggling with handwritten sketches
- Losing deals to faster competitors
- Wasted time on leads that never convert
- Manual processes taking 2-3 hours per quote

**The Question:** What if quotes could happen instantly—before the bakkie ever leaves the shop?

---

## SLIDE 2: THE SOLUTION

**Introducing The OWD Glass-Engine**

A 100% WhatsApp-native AI Auto-Estimator that replaces the manual estimator. 

Customers simply send a photo of a rough sketch or text message with measurements. The AI instantly converts it into professional, SANS-compliant PDF quotes—collecting deposits automatically.

**No apps. No forms. No friction.** Just WhatsApp—the app your customers already use.

---

## SLIDE 3: THREE AI MODULES

**Your Automated Staff Member**

We don't sell a "chatbot." We provide an AI Staff Member handling three distinct functions:

**🪟 The Estimator** — Handles high-volume glass window and door quotes. Reads handwritten sketches via Google Gemini Vision OCR and generates professional PDF quotes in under 5 minutes.

**🚿 The Closer** — High-value shower enclosure specialist. Analyzes bathroom photos, provides technical assessments with SANS compliance, and collects survey deposits before site visits.

**🔧 The Filter** — Window repair triage. Analyzes damage photos, detects safety requirements, and collects call-out fees before dispatching technicians.

---

## SLIDE 4: HOW IT WORKS

**The Customer Journey (60 Seconds)**

1. **Customer sends photo** of sketch or bathroom via WhatsApp
2. **AI extracts data** — dimensions, glass types, frame colors, quantities
3. **Confirmation sent** — Customer taps "YES" to verify measurements (liability shifts to customer)
4. **PDF generated** — Professional branded quote delivered instantly
5. **Payment collected** — Deposit or survey fee collected via integrated payment link

**All before your bakkie leaves the shop.**

---

## SLIDE 5: THE TECHNOLOGY

**Enterprise-Grade Architecture**

- **WhatsApp API:** Official WhatsApp Business API (BotSailor)
- **AI Engine:** Google Gemini (Vision OCR + Analysis)
- **Workflow Automation:** n8n (enterprise orchestration)
- **Database:** Supabase (PostgreSQL with real-time updates)
- **Payment Integration:** PayFast / Yoco
- **Distance Calculation:** Google Maps API
- **Web Dashboard:** Vercel-hosted React application

**Data Flow:** WhatsApp → AI Analysis → Quote Calculation → PDF Generation → Payment → Customer Response (all automated)

## SLIDE 6: WHY OWD WINS

**Competitive Advantages**

**Liability Protection:** Customer confirms measurements on their own phone. System records: "Measurements confirmed by customer." Shifts liability from glass shop to customer.
