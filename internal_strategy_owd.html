5. Critical Architecture: The "Developer's Edge"

As the core architect, these three technical functions remain the "moat" that competitors cannot easily copy:

  • Liability Shifting: Measurements are parsed but explicitly confirmed by the customer via the web-dashboard/WhatsApp UI. "Measurements confirmed by customer." Protected liability.
  • SANS Guardrails: Automatic flagging if a customer wants 4mm glass where 6.38mm safety glass is required under SANS 10400-N.
  • Dynamic Database Management: n8n pulls from Supabase. When glass suppliers raise prices by 8%, the client's whole quoting system updates via one DB change.
