Scope of Work & Conditions

To ensure a successful and transparent partnership, the following parameters clearly define our offering:

  • Standard Integration: The setup fee covers the precise configuration of up to 5 WhatsApp chat flows (e.g., Showers, Repairs). Additional custom pathways or CRM integrations beyond the initial scope will be quoted separately.
  • Platform Environment: Our automation operates within the established frameworks of the official WhatsApp API, BotSailor, and our backend logic engine. It is a dedicated quote-automation tool, not a completely custom-coded ERP software suite.
  • Pricing & Liability: The AI constructs quotes based strictly on the pricing matrices you provide. While the customer verifies sizes via the dashboard before the quote is generated, final responsibility for quality control, site installation, and honoring prices rests entirely with your business.
  • WhatsApp API Fees: The monthly subscription (R6,000) covers our proprietary AI processing, workflow hosting, system maintenance, and support. It does not cover Meta's (Facebook) direct per-conversation API messaging fees, which are nominally billed directly to your business account by Meta.
  • Maintenance & Support: The monthly fee includes bug fixes, server uptime, AI model fine-tuning, and priority technical support during standard business hours. Structural changes or entirely new feature developments are treated as separate projects.
